THE RESIDENCE

ARCHITECTURE THAT FEELS DIFFERENT.

Hovering weightlessly above the limestone banks of Lake Austin, The Glass Residence commands a quiet, meditative authority. Designed as an interconnected series of cantilevering post-tensioned concrete trays and full-height acoustic glazing, the home achieves a rare equilibrium: monumental, raw brutalist materiality paired with delicate transparency. Every angle frames uninterrupted panoramas of still water and ancient cedar live oaks.

Interior spaces celebrate spatial discipline. Polished charcoal basalt flooring grounds the pavilion, catching subtle gradients of dawn and dusk sunlight filtered through motorized architectural louvers. Custom architectural bronze hardware, seamlessly integrated fluted travertine walls, and concealed mechanical tracks ensure that nothing distracts from the pure sensation of floating between sky and water.

Living zones flow outdoors onto a 1,420-square-foot sheltered terrace and zero-edge black pebble reflection pool. A private serpentine limestone path meanders through native desert agave and sotol down to the private deep-water dock, providing immediate Lake Austin marine access while remaining thoroughly shielded from public vantage points.
