## Brand & Style

This design system embodies ultra-prime architectural luxury, targeting discerning ultra-high-net-worth individuals, premier property collectors, and world-class architects. The aesthetic evokes the atmosphere of a museum monograph or a private architectural sanctuary at dusk: quiet, commanding, authoritative, and utterly devoid of transient digital trends.

The design movement blends **Architectural Minimalism** with **Editorial Brutalism**:
- Razor-sharp discipline with generous negative space, mirroring modern concrete and charred timber estates.
- Monochromatic foundation built on deep charcoals, near-black voids, and warm chalk whites, energized solely by warm amber architectural light touches.
- Zero rounded visual fluff: strictly rectilinear structures, ultra-thin 1px tectonic hairline borders, and full-bleed high-fidelity imagery.
- Typographic tension: bold, geometric sans-serif statements juxtaposed against restrained, tracked-out micro-labels.

## Colors

The color system is engineered for dark mode default, inspired by dusk-lit brutalist architecture and night architectural photography:

- **Primary (`#F7F7F4` - Warm Chalk White):** The primary illumination token for headlines, key active states, and pristine foreground elements. 
- **Secondary (`#D4A373` - Warm Amber Luminescence):** A restrained architectural accent reminiscent of warm interior spotlighting penetrating dark facades. Used sparingly for interactive highlights, curated micro-tags, and focus outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#171817` - Deep Charcoal):** The structural surface tier used for elevated planes, card backgrounds, hover overlays, and segmented controls.
- **Neutral (`#090A09` - Obsidian Ground):** The infinite canvas background, grounding layouts in total tonal stability.
- **Muted Gray (`#B7B6B0`):** Applied exclusively for secondary metadata, reading copy, hairline perimeter dividers (`rgba(183, 182, 176, 0.15)`), and non-interactive framing.

Gradients of any neon or saturated variety are prohibited; subtle radial glows are restricted purely to simulating 2700K architectural ambient light sources behind imagery.

## Typography

Typography delivers a cinematic, editorial balance. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Contemporary, bold, structured, and deliberate. Rendered in full uppercase for lead hero banners (`LIVE WHERE LUXURY RESIDES`) and title lockups, reinforcing monumentality.
- **Body & Long-form (`Manrope`):** Calm, geometric yet humanist proportions offering effortless legibility against dark surfaces without vibrating against high contrast.
- **Micro & Overlines (`label-caps`):** Strict uppercase formatting paired with aggressive tracking (`0.18em`) creates architectural rhythm above sections, property stats, and category badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural grid with deep breathing room:

- **12-Column Fluid Frame:** Responsive canvas capped at a max-width of 1728px, floating within expansive outer margins (`4rem` on desktop). On mobile devices (below 768px), it collapses into a 4-column system with tight `1.25rem` margins to maximize cinematic image real estate.
- **Hero Staging:** Key hero viewports occupy an framed container (mimicking the exterior window framing of modern estates), setting hero typography flush left with generous vertical offsets.
- **Rhythm:** Macro spacing between sections leans intentionally generous (`4rem` to `8rem`), allowing each estate or feature to exist as an independent gallery piece.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects drop shadows entirely. Depth is structured through structural layering and lighting:

- **Tonal Stepping:**
  - Base layer: `#090A09` (Obsidian ground).
  - Elevated surfaces: `#171817` (Deep Charcoal containers).
  - Hover/Active states: `#202220` (Subtle charcoal highlight).
- **Hairline Tectonic Borders:** Separation is handled exclusively via crisp 1px borders using `rgba(183, 182, 176, 0.16)` or `rgba(247, 247, 244, 0.12)`. No blurred or heavy borders.
- **Glass Transparency:** Navigation bars and floating panels employ ultra-dark translucent glass: `rgba(9, 10, 9, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a subtle 1px bottom border.
- **Amber Glow Accents:** Interior room illumination or featured properties utilize localized ambient backdrops (`radial-gradient(ellipse at center, rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.08) 0%, transparent 70%)`) to create tactile warmth.

## Shapes

The shape system is absolute and uncompromising: **Sharp (Level 0)**.

- All interactive containers, buttons, cards, imagery viewports, and modals utilize pure 90-degree right angles (`border-radius: 0px`).
- The strict rectangular language mimics the cantilevered planes, board-formed concrete, and horizontal expanses of contemporary architecture.
- Circular or pill shapes are forbidden, except for standard micro-indicators like scroll indicators or functional status dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Monolith Solid):** Background `#F7F7F4`, text `#090A09`, font `Space Grotesk` medium, `font-size: 0.8125rem`, uppercase with tracking `0.12em`. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Zero radius. Hover shifts to `#D4A373` with instant 150ms ease.
- **Secondary Action (Hairline Wire):** Transparent background, 1px border `rgba(247, 247, 244, 0.35)`, text `#F7F7F4`. Hover shifts border and text to `#D4A373` with subtle background tint `rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.05)`.
- **Text Link:** Uppercase `label-caps` with a 1px baseline underline offset by `6px`, expanding in width on hover.

### Navigation Header
- Floating or fixed layout with translucent obsidian ground (`rgba(9, 10, 9, 0.75)`), blurred backdrop, and a hairline bottom rule.
- Monogram/Wordmark set in bold `Space Grotesk` uppercase. Menu items in `Space Grotesk` caps with hover transitions from `#B7B6B0` to `#F7F7F4`.

### Property Cards
- Sharp rectangular modules with 1px border `rgba(183, 182, 176, 0.14)`.
- Image fills the upper compartment with an aspect ratio of 16:10. Subtle zoom effect (scale 1.02) on hover without rounded corners.
- Card footer contains property name in `Space Grotesk`, pricing in amber `#D4A373`, and key architectural specs (sq ft, beds, location) separated by 1px vertical hairpins.

### Inputs & Fields
- Dark planar style: background `#171817`, 1px border `rgba(183, 182, 176, 0.25)`, zero radius.
- Text input in `#F7F7F4` with placeholders in `#B7B6B0`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D4A373` with zero outward ring/shadow.

### Chips & Overlines
- Rectangular pill badges are prohibited. Tags are structured as razor-sharp micro-boxes: 1px border `rgba(183, 182, 176, 0.2)`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`, typography `label-caps`, uppercase.

### Gallery & Media Thumbnails
- Embedded bottom-corner carousels featuring sharp 1px border thumbnails with an active indicator frame rendered in solid warm amber (`#D4A373`).
